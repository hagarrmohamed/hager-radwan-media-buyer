Case studies

Real campaigns, real results

Three projects, each with the problem, what I changed, and the outcome. Ad names in the Ads Manager screenshots are hidden for client privacy; all figures are unedited.

Healthcare · Lead generation

Physical Therapy Clinic

9xROAS

The challenge

The client had several problems that hurt lead quality and wasted ad spend.

The results

After fixing these problems and improving targeting and lead quality, we reached a 9x ROAS.

The solution

  1. High pricing. Medical add-ons made the price higher than competitors' prices. We lowered the base price to be more competitive and showed patients each add-on and its cost separately.
  2. Wrong audience. We stopped targeting people interested in insurance. Instead, we focused on freelancers and employees of small companies.
  3. Repeated questions about location. We showed the clinic's location clearly in the ads and added it to the auto-replies. This cut down on people asking the same question again and again.
  4. Low-quality leads. We built a Custom Audience of people who start a chat but don't follow up, then excluded them from targeting to get better-quality messages.

Healthcare · Dental implants

Dental Clinic

8xROAS

The challenge

Dental implants are expensive. Before people book, they need to trust the quality of the service and feel it's worth the price.

The results

The campaign reached an 8x ROAS.

The solution

  1. Audience testing. I tested 4 different audiences with two offers: a discount and a payment plan, to find out which audience was most interested and which offer worked best for each group.
  2. Optimization. The results showed that people planning to get married were more interested in the service than older people, and that each audience responded differently to the offers.
  3. Scaling. I stopped the weaker ads and moved the budget to the ads that brought more bookings at a lower cost. I also made new creatives for the best audience.

E-commerce · Jordan market

Home Wear Brand

7xROAS

The challenge

The brand has a lot of different products. The client needed to find the best-performing products so they could import more of them.

The results

We used the performance data to decide where to spend the budget and which products to import first. In the end, we reached a 7x ROAS.

The solution

  1. We started with a Catalog Campaign to test all the products and find the most promising ones.
  2. We tested the top products with the same budget and the same targeting, so we could compare their performance fairly.
  3. We kept testing, step by step, until we had tested 50 products. Then we picked the top 15 and spent most of the ad budget on them.
