Case 01

Physical Therapy Clinic

9×ROAS

The challenge

The client had several problems that hurt lead quality and wasted ad spend.

Top ad, messaging conversations
615
Lowest cost per conversation shown
5.20 EGP
ProblemWhat changed

  1. 01 High pricing

    Medical add-ons made the price higher than competitors' prices.

    Lowered the base price to be more competitive, and showed patients each add-on and its cost separately.

  2. 02 Wrong audience

    Ads were reaching people interested in insurance.

    Stopped targeting them and focused on freelancers and employees of small companies.

  3. 03 Repeated questions about location

    People kept asking where the clinic was.

    Showed the clinic's location clearly in the ads and added it to the auto-replies, which cut down the repeated questions.

  4. 04 Low-quality leads

    Many people started a chat but didn't follow up.

    Built a Custom Audience of those people and excluded it from targeting to get better-quality messages.

The result After fixing these problems and improving targeting and lead quality, the account reached 9x ROAS.

Case 02

Dental Clinic

8×ROAS

The challenge

Dental implants are expensive. Before people book, they need to trust the quality of the service and feel it's worth the price.

  1. 01 / Test

    Audience testing

    Tested 4 different audiences with two offers, a discount and a payment plan, to find which audience was most interested and which offer worked best for each group.

  2. 02 / Read

    Optimization

    People planning to get married were more interested in the service than older people, and each audience responded differently to the offers.

  3. 03 / Scale

    Scaling

    Stopped the weaker ads, moved the budget to the ads that brought more bookings at a lower cost, and made new creatives for the best audience.

Case 03

Home Wear Brand Jordan market

7×ROAS

The challenge

The brand has a lot of different products. The client needed to find the best-performing ones so they could import more of them.

  1. 01

    Started with a Catalog Campaign to test all the products and find the most promising ones.

  2. 02

    Tested the top products with the same budget and the same targeting, so their performance could be compared fairly.

  3. 03

    Kept testing step by step until 50 products were tested, then picked the top 15 and spent most of the budget on them.
